Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Sentinel Habits is a task, reminder, timer, and stopwatch app for iOS. This policy explains what data the app does — and does not — handle.

What's stored, and where

Everything you enter — tasks, reminders, timers, stopwatches, completion history, and household member names — is stored only in a local database on your own device. None of it is sent to us or to any third party, and we (the developer) have no access to it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

No data collection, no analytics, no ads

Sentinel Habits does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us or any third party. The app has no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

Notifications

The app uses Apple's local notification system to remind you about tasks and alert you when a timer finishes. These notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device — nothing is sent through a remote push service or an external server.

No accounts, no tracking

The app does not require sign-in, does not use cookies or device identifiers for tracking, and does not communicate with any server.

Children's privacy

Sentinel Habits does not knowingly collect information from children, and does not collect information from anyone — all data stays on-device as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect it.
