Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Sentinel Shop is a shopping trip and grocery list app for iOS. This policy explains what data the app does — and does not — handle.

What's stored, and where

Everything you enter — shopping trips, items, quantities, prices, and any custom catalog items you add — is stored only in a local database on your own device. None of it is sent to us or to any third party, and we (the developer) have no access to it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

No data collection, no analytics, no ads

Sentinel Shop does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with us or any third party. The app has no network access, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

Backup and export

The app lets you export a backup of your data to a file of your choosing, for example to save to iCloud Drive — this is initiated entirely by you and stays under your control. We never receive a copy.

No accounts, no tracking

The app does not require sign-in, does not use cookies or device identifiers for tracking, and does not communicate with any server.

Children's privacy

Sentinel Shop does not knowingly collect information from children, and does not collect information from anyone — all data stays on-device as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect it.
