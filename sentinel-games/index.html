Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Sentinel Games is an offline collection of classic games and puzzles for iOS. This policy explains what data the app does — and does not — handle.

No data collection

Sentinel Games does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no network access, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

What's stored, and where

Any information you enter — player names, game history, scores, and settings — is stored only in a local database on your own device. It is never sent anywhere, and we (the developer) have no access to it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

No accounts, no tracking

The app does not require sign-in, does not use cookies or device identifiers for tracking, and does not communicate with any server.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data of any kind, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect it.
