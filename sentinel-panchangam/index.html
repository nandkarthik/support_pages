Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Sentinel Panchangam is an offline Telugu almanac app for iOS. This policy explains what data the app does — and does not — handle.

No data collection

Sentinel Panchangam does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no network access, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. All panchangam, festival, and muhurta calculations are performed on your device.

What's stored, and where

Your selected location, any people you add (for personalized Tara Bala timings), your appearance preference, and other settings are stored only in local storage on your own device. This information is never sent anywhere, and we (the developer) have no access to it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

No accounts, no tracking

The app does not require sign-in, does not use GPS or device location services (location is chosen from a fixed list of cities), and does not use cookies or device identifiers for tracking.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data of any kind, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect it.
